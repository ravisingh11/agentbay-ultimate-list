# Community conduct

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests and project discussions. Contributions should make the catalogue useful and trustworthy for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Discuss evidence, behavior and the proposed change. Be respectful when disagreeing and allow others time to respond.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or repeatedly target another participant.
- Do not publish someone else's private information, credentials, private prompts or account data.
- Avoid spam and deceptive promotion. Disclose material affiliations when proposing your own project; inclusion does not imply endorsement.

Maintainers may request changes, edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. Reports are considered in context; maintainers may ask for more information and give participants an opportunity to respond where appropriate.

Report a conduct concern privately to [hello@agentbay.io](mailto:hello@agentbay.io), with links to the relevant interaction and the smallest necessary description. Do not repost harmful or private material in a public issue. Maintainers handle reports with discretion; response times depend on availability, and no service-level commitment is implied.

For a vulnerability or credential exposure, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For an ordinary listing correction or installer problem, use the repository's [issue forms](https://github.com/ravisingh11/agentbay-ultimate-list/issues/new/choose).
